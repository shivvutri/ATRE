Important Date Application Fee Payment की Last Date — 15 October 2026
General/OBC/EWS: ₹1,000 | SC/ST: ₹500 | PwBD: ₹300। Fee Debit Card, Credit Card या Net Banking से pay करें। Application Correction Window: 19 October 2026 तक खुली रहेगी।
3–4 Dec 2026
Exam Date Written Exam (Chayan Pariksha) — 3 & 4 December 2026 (Tentative)
Exam 360 marks का होगा — 120 MCQ (Objective Type), 2 घंटे की अवधि। Correct answer: +3 marks | Wrong answer: –1 mark (Negative Marking)। Exam bilingual (Hindi & English) होगा। Multiple shifts होने पर Normalization लागू होगा।
Sep 2026
Eligibility Eligibility: Graduation + B.T.C./D.El.Ed. + TET Qualified (Age: 21–40 वर्ष)
Urban Primary Post के लिए: Graduate degree + B.T.C. / D.El.Ed. (NCTE recognised) + UP या Central TET (Class 1–5) qualify होना अनिवार्य। Age limit: 21–40 वर्ष (01.07.2026 के अनुसार)। SC/ST/OBC को 5 वर्ष, PwBD को 15 वर्ष की छूट। Salary: Pay Level-6 — ₹35,400–₹1,12,400 (7th CPC)।
